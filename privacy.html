Privacy

Jyotish Guide is built for learning. We treat birth details and chart inputs as sensitive personal information.

Birth data stays local

When you use chart or study tools associated with this project, birth date, time, and place are intended to remain on your own device for calculation and display. We do not ask you to upload birth data to this static website, and this landing page itself does not collect or store birth charts.

Educational, not medical

Content on this site is educational and cultural. It is not medical advice, mental-health treatment, legal counsel, or financial guidance. Do not use Jyotish content as a substitute for licensed professional care.

This static site

This GitHub Pages site serves HTML, CSS, and public links. It does not run a backend that logs your birth data. Third-party sites you open (for example Amazon or Archive.org) have their own privacy policies.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon search links on the books page include an Associates tracking tag.

Contact

For questions about this privacy notice, use the contact channel listed on the Jyotish Guide project or repository associated with this site.
