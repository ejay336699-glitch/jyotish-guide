What is Jyotish Guide?

Jyotish Guide is a tool that teaches you Vedic astrology. It is built to feel straightforward: you open a chart and it walks you through the layers, from signs and houses to nakshatras and dashas, and it pulls that old system down into something you can actually use.

The app is not free to use. Birth data you enter is meant to stay on your device for study. We do not offer medical diagnoses, therapy, legal counsel, or investment guidance.
