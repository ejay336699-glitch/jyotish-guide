What is Jyotish Guide?

Jyotish Guide is an educational project for people who want to understand Vedic astrology on its own terms. We focus on core concepts — the twelve signs (rāśis), nine grahas, houses (bhāvas), nakṣatras, and timing systems such as Vimśottarī daśā — with explanations you can revisit at your own pace.

The app is not free to use. Birth data you enter is meant to stay on your device for study. We do not offer medical diagnoses, therapy, legal counsel, or investment guidance.

P. D. (or standard scholarly) editions of Bṛhat Jātaka remain a cornerstone of natal Jyotish. Read a public-domain or scan edition on Archive.org, then keep a print copy for annotation if you prefer paper.
